# Rendyn SEO audit and release — 5 September 2026

The SEO repairs are deployed to https://rendyn.dev. Google Search Console confirmed that its saved homepage HTML still contained **DesignMCP**, while the live site serves **Rendyn | React UI Components for AI Coding Agents**. Google accepted a fresh indexing request for the homepage and `/guides`. The sitemap submission succeeded with **205 discovered pages**. Search-result title refresh and actual guide indexing still depend on Google's next crawl.

The release preserves the Horizon design and preview fixes committed to main during this audit. Both histories were integrated with a merge commit, without force-pushing or rewriting commits.

## Production checks

The final integrated crawl covered **205 sitemap pages plus 10 extra destinations**. All sitemap pages returned HTTP 200, with exactly one H1, a matching HTTPS Rendyn canonical, unique title and description, Rendyn social identity, no `noindex` and no DesignMCP/old-host references. Root URLs with and without a trailing slash are normalized for canonical comparison. The 189 component pages retain their existing routes. Descriptions are at most 159 characters; this is an editorial choice, not a Google character-limit requirement.

- Robots permits public crawling and advertises the correct sitemap. API/authentication handlers remain excluded.
- Login, consent and valid embedded previews use `noindex, nofollow`; account redirects to login. These are excluded from the sitemap.
- Three deliberately invalid routes returned real 404 responses; they are not missing public content.
- The legacy ChatGPT website guide redirects with 308 to its current guide URL.
- Filtered catalogue URLs canonicalize to `/components`.
- HTTP apex redirects to HTTPS. HTTP and HTTPS www redirect to HTTPS apex, preserving `/guides?seo_check=1` and component paths.
- Favicon, Rendyn social image and both self-hosted font assets return 200.
- Homepage WebSite/Organization and catalogue CollectionPage/ItemList JSON-LD parse successfully. No fabricated ratings or reviews were added.
- Compiled homepage browser smoke check passed with the current Horizon design and no console errors.

Initial per-page evidence is retained in [seo-audit-2026-09-05.json](./seo-audit-2026-09-05.json). Final production metadata and infrastructure evidence is in [seo-release-2026-09-05.json](./seo-release-2026-09-05.json).
